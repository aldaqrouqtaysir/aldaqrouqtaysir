# Taysir Al Daqrouq

I’m a Computer Science student at United Arab Emirates University and an American raised in the UAE. I’m working toward becoming a research engineer, with interests in reliable machine learning, statistical computing, and research software.

I care about whether a result holds up—not just whether a model runs. My approach is to define the question clearly, start with strong baselines, control for leakage, make the work reproducible, and state limitations plainly. I’m especially interested in environmental and public-service problems where the data is imperfect and the conclusions need careful evaluation.

## Selected work

- **[Dubai PM₂.₅ Cross-Site Forecasting](https://github.com/aldaqrouqtaysir/dubai-pm25-cross-site-forecasting)** — Evaluates whether forecasting models transfer across four Dubai sensor sites using leave-one-site-out testing and leakage-controlled validation.
- **[Inferential Safety Lab](https://github.com/aldaqrouqtaysir/inferential-safety-lab)** — Uses deterministic, known-answer simulations to compare statistical-analysis pipelines under missingness and measurement error.
- **[SAIS Academic Navigator](https://github.com/aldaqrouqtaysir/academic-pathfinder-app)** — A rule-based academic planner that evaluates up to 1,728 schedules from a 57-course catalog.
